Carrel is a desktop reader for the open web. You can read locally, save passages
and take notes without setting up sync or adding a person. These pages describe
the current pre-alpha; the [vision](https://github.com/cigrainger/carrel/blob/main/docs/VISION.md)
looks further ahead.
